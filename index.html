Forced Browsing

The Setup Assume we have a browser-based storage application similar to Google Drive or OneDrive in front of us. As the user Christoph Schubert you might see this URL in your address bar when looking at the folder documents/bank-statements: https://www.my-webstorage.com/christoph-schubert/documents/bank-statements The Attack Taking a deeper look at this URL you can see the username christoph-schubert in there. An attacker will now try to replace this valid username with other values, hoping that they might be able to see the data of that new, guessed user.
